Privacy

The waitlist

When you join the waitlist we save the email or phone number you typed, the time you joined, and which link you came from. We use it only to tell you when chungi is ready. We don't sell it or share it with anyone.

Analytics

This site can count visits with Umami when the person running it turns it on. Umami records the page you viewed, the site that sent you, your browser, device type and country, and clicks on things like the waitlist button and the demo screens. It uses no cookies and doesn't store your IP address.

Getting removed

Reply to any message we send you and ask, and we'll delete your details from the waitlist.
